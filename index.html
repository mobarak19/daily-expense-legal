FAQ

Common questions

Is Daily Expenses free?

Yes. Logging expenses, budgets, categories, recurring expenses, widgets, reminders and CSV export are free. Pro is an optional upgrade that removes ads and adds extra features.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Open the app and start logging.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in the app's local database. It isn't uploaded to our servers. It's included in your regular iCloud or computer backup like any other app's data.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings → Export Data creates CSV files for your expenses, categories, recurring expenses, IOUs, investments and upcoming expenses. Pro users can also export PDF and Word reports.

Which currencies are supported?

US Dollar, Euro, British Pound, Bangladeshi Taka and Indian Rupee. You can change the currency in Settings.
