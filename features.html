Features

Everything Daily Expenses can do

The full list, including the small details. Features marked PRO need the optional Pro upgrade. Everything else is free.

Home dashboard

Your month on one screen, so you can see where you stand without tapping anywhere.

  • Today and This MonthTwo cards showing what you've spent today and this month, and how many transactions.
  • Monthly budget cardHow much you've spent, what's left, and how much you're over if you pass your budget.
  • Recent transactionsYour last 5 expenses, with a "See All" link to your full history.
  • Upcoming expensesYour next planned bills, with the soonest one marked "Next".
  • IOU and investment cardsYour net IOU position and investment gain or loss at a glance.
  • Quick add buttonOne floating button to add an expense or a new category.
  • Notification inboxA bell with an unread count, right from Home.
  • Friendly empty statesClear guidance when you haven't added anything yet.

Adding and editing expenses

Logging should take seconds, and fixing a mistake should be just as quick.

  • Quick entryAmount, title, category and date. That's it.
  • New category on the spotCreate a category from the Add Expense screen without leaving it.
  • Works with your number formatType amounts with a dot or a comma. Both work.
  • Repeat this expenseTurn any expense into a daily, weekly or monthly repeating one.
  • Edit anythingChange the amount, note, category or date later.
  • DuplicateAdd the same expense again with today's date, which is handy for repeat purchases.
  • Share an expenseSend a short text summary of an expense to anyone.
  • Safe deleteDeleting asks you to confirm first.

Expense history

Every expense you've logged, easy to search, filter and export.

  • SearchFind expenses by title or category.
  • FiltersFilter by category, month and year, or view all of them at once.
  • Grouped by dayToday, Yesterday, then each date, so the list is easy to scan.
  • Filtered totalSee the total spent for whatever you've filtered.
  • Swipe to deleteRemove an expense straight from the list.
  • CSV export and text summaryShare your filtered expenses as a CSV file or a plain-text summary.
  • PDF and Word reports PROCreate a formatted report of your expenses to save or share.

Categories

Organize spending the way you think about it.

  • Ready-made categoriesFood & Drink, Transport, Groceries, Entertainment, Shopping and Income, ready from the start.
  • Custom categoriesChoose a name, icon and color, and see a live preview.
  • Budget per categoryGive any category its own monthly budget.
  • All Categories screenSearch your categories and see them split into budgeted and not budgeted, with a monthly total.
  • Protected from mistakesYou can't delete your last category, or one that a scheduled expense still uses.

Budgets and alerts

Plan your month and get a warning before you overspend, not after.

  • Total monthly budgetOne number for everything you plan to spend this month.
  • Category budgetsSplit your budget across categories.
  • Unallocated amountSee how much of your budget isn't assigned to a category yet, or how much you've over-assigned.
  • Budget alertsGet a notification when you reach the percentage you choose, for the whole budget or a category.
  • Per-category alert level PROFor example, be warned at 60% for dining out but at 90% for groceries.

Statistics

Charts that show your spending patterns.

  • Week and month viewsSwitch periods to see recent spending.
  • Spending trend chartSee how your spending changes day by day.
  • Category breakdownSee how your spending is split across categories.
  • TotalsTotal spent and your average spending per day.
  • Top categoriesYour top 3 categories for free, or the full ranking with Pro.
  • Year view and past years PROLook back across the whole year and at previous years.
  • Vs. last month PROSee how this month compares with the last one.
  • Quick insights PROKey facts like your top category, pulled out for you.
  • PDF report PROExport your statistics as a PDF.

Upcoming expenses

Plan future bills so they don't catch you by surprise.

  • Plan aheadAdd an amount, title, category and due date for any future bill.
  • RepeatsNever, weekly, monthly or yearly.
  • Due-date remindersGet a notification on the day it's due.
  • Mark as paidOne tap turns it into a real expense, and repeating bills move to their next date.
  • 3 free, unlimited with ProFree users can plan 3 upcoming expenses at a time.

Recurring expenses

Subscriptions and regular payments that log themselves.

  • Automatic loggingA daily, weekly or monthly expense is added for you each time it comes due.
  • Catches upIf the app was closed on the due date, it adds the missed expenses the next time you open it.
  • Notified when addedYou get a notification each time a recurring expense is logged.
  • Optional end dateStop a repeat automatically, for example when a plan ends.
  • Manage in one placeChange the amount, note or frequency of any recurring expense, or delete it.

People & IOUs

Keep track of money you lent to friends or borrowed from them, or from a bank.

  • Lent and borrowedRecord who owes whom, how much, and when.
  • Summary"You're owed" and "You owe" totals.
  • InterestNo interest, simple or compound, with an annual rate. The amount owed updates over time.
  • Due dates and remindersGet a reminder on the due date, and overdue IOUs are clearly marked.
  • Swipe to settleSwipe right to mark an IOU as settled, swipe left to delete it.
  • Settled historySettled IOUs move to their own history screen, where you can review them.
  • Kept separateIOUs never change your spending totals or budget.

Investments

A simple record of what you've invested and what it's worth now.

  • Many investment typesStocks, mutual funds, crypto, fixed deposits, real estate, gold and more.
  • Invested vs. current valueTotal invested, current value and gain or loss, with the percentage return.
  • Update values any timeOpen an investment and enter its latest value.
  • Filter by typeShow only stocks, only crypto, and so on.

Widgets

See what you can still spend today without opening the app.

  • Home Screen widgetSmall, medium and large sizes showing what's left today, whether you're on pace, this week's spending and your top categories.
  • Lock Screen widgetCircular, rectangular and inline styles, including one that reads "$27 left today" above the clock.
  • Quick AddA Lock Screen button that opens Add Expense.
  • Private on the Lock ScreenAmounts stay hidden until you unlock your iPhone.
  • Resets at midnight"Today" starts over at midnight, even if you don't open the app.

Notifications

Reminders that help you keep logging, without too many interruptions.

  • Daily reminderAt the time you choose, with today's spending compared with your usual day.
  • Weekly and monthly summariesA regular recap of how much you spent.
  • Budget alertsFor your total budget and for each category.
  • Bill, IOU and recurring remindersOn due dates, and whenever a recurring expense is added.
  • Free trial reminderA heads-up before a Pro free trial turns into a paid subscription.
  • In-app inboxPast notifications with unread markers, Mark All Read, swipe to delete and Clear All.

Security

Keep your financial information private, even if someone else picks up your phone.

  • App Lock PROFace ID, Touch ID or your passcode is needed to open the app. It locks again when you leave.
  • Feature Lock PROLock only certain screens: Statistics, Investments, IOUs, Expense History, Upcoming or Recurring Expenses, or Clear All Data.
  • Changes need youTurning a lock on or off always asks you to authenticate first.
  • Biometrics stay with AppleFace ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS. The app never sees your face or fingerprint data.

Settings, data and support

Make the app your own, and stay in control of your data.

  • CurrencyUSD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), BDT (৳) or INR (₹).
  • AppearanceSystem, light or dark mode.
  • First day of the weekChoose any day, and weekly views follow it.
  • Export all dataCSV files for expenses, categories, recurring expenses, IOUs, investments and upcoming expenses.
  • Clear all dataStart over in one step, with a confirmation first.
  • Contact supportEmail us from inside the app.
  • Diagnostic reportsIf the app crashes, you can choose to send us the crash report so we can fix it. It's only sent if you share it.
  • Ad privacy optionsReview or change your ad consent choices at any time.
  • No ads with Pro PROPro removes all ads from the app.
