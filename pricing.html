Billing questions

Good to know

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers to the yearly plan get 1 week free. The app sends you a reminder 2 days before the trial ends. If you cancel before the trial ends, you won't be charged.

How do I cancel?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Daily Expenses Pro. Apple handles all billing, so we can't cancel it for you.

I got a new iPhone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Daily Expenses, tap Upgrade, then tap Restore Purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Your expenses and settings stay on your device, and Pro features lock again until you resubscribe.
