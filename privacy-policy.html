Privacy Policy

How Daily Expenses handles your information

Effective September 28, 2026v2.0

Daily Expenses is built to track your spending, not you. You don't need an account, and your financial records are stored only on your iPhone. This policy explains in plain language what information the app uses, where it's stored, and what little leaves your device.

§01Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Daily Expenses iOS app and its widgets ("the App"), developed by Mobarak Hossen ("we," "us"). It also covers this website. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

In short:

  • Your expenses, budgets and other records stay on your device. We don't run servers that receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described in §04. Pro users don't see ads.
  • We only receive information from you if you choose to send it, for example by emailing us.

§02Data Stored on Your Device

The App creates and stores the following on your device only, in a local database:

TypeExamples
Financial entries you addExpenses, categories, monthly and category budgets, recurring expenses, upcoming expenses
Other records you addIOUs (people's names, amounts, dates, interest, notes) and investments
App preferencesCurrency, appearance, first day of the week, notification and lock settings
In-app notification historyPast budget alerts, reminders and tips shown in the inbox
Purchase statusWhether you have Pro, as verified by Apple
Crash diagnosticsTechnical crash and hang reports provided by iOS (see §05)

None of this requires an account, and none of it is sent to us. The App doesn't sync to the cloud. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the App's data is included in that backup like any other app's data, under Apple's terms.

If you enter other people's names in IOUs, that information also stays on your device.

§03What We Don't Collect

  • No sign-up, name, email address or password
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, apart from the advertising SDK described in §04
  • We never sell your data, and we never share your financial records with anyone

§04Advertising (Free Version)

To keep the App free, the free version shows banner and full-screen ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Pro users don't see ads, and the ad SDK doesn't load ads for them.

What Google may collect

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device information, such as model, iOS version, language and screen size
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (for example, your country)
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below)

This data goes directly from the SDK to Google. We don't receive it. Your expenses, budgets and other records are never shared with Google or any ad network. Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

Before iOS asks whether the App may track you, the App shows a short explanation first. If you choose Allow, Google can use your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier isn't shared, and you may still see ads that are less personalized. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where the law requires it, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy Options.

§05Crash Diagnostics

The App uses Apple's MetricKit framework, not a third-party service, to receive crash and hang reports from iOS. These reports contain technical details, such as which part of the code failed and your device model and iOS version. They don't contain your expenses or other records.

The App keeps up to 20 of the most recent reports on your device. They are only sent to us if you choose to share them, using Settings → Export Diagnostic Reports. Separately, if you've turned on "Share with App Developers" in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send anonymous crash data to us.

§06Purchases

Pro subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The App asks Apple whether your Apple ID has an active purchase so it can turn on Pro features, and it may schedule a local reminder before a free trial ends.

§07Face ID, Widgets & Notifications

Face ID / Touch ID

App Lock and Feature Lock use Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. iOS does the check, and the App only learns whether it succeeded. The App never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Widgets

To power the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the App saves a small summary on your device (your recent expenses, budget and currency). Only the widgets can read it. It never leaves your device. Lock Screen widgets hide amounts until your iPhone is unlocked.

Notifications

Reminders, budget alerts, spending summaries and due-date reminders are created and scheduled on your device, based on your own data and settings. They aren't sent from a server. You can turn them off in the App's Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

§08Exports & Support Email

Exports. When you export CSV, PDF or Word files, or share an expense summary, the App creates the file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next, whether Files, Mail or another app, is your choice and is subject to that service's privacy policy.

Support email. If you contact us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, such as screenshots or diagnostic reports. We use this only to reply to you and to fix problems, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

§09Your Choices & Rights

  • Ads: change your tracking permission in iPhone Settings, review your ad consent in Settings → Privacy Options, or upgrade to Pro to remove ads.
  • Notifications: turn any reminder on or off in the App or in iPhone Settings.
  • Access and export: export all your data as CSV files at any time.
  • Deletion: use Settings → Clear All Data, or delete the App.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU and UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because your records stay on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For support emails you've sent us, contact us and we'll help. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myadcenter.google.com.

§10Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it with Clear All Data or delete the App, which removes its local database and widget data. Copies in your own device backups follow your backup settings. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

§11Children

The App isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

§12Changes to This Policy

When this policy changes, we'll update the effective date and version number at the top of this page. Important changes, such as new data collection, cloud sync or a new third-party service, will be clearly described here before they take effect.

What's new in v2.0: added details on Google AdMob advertising, App Tracking Transparency and consent, MetricKit crash diagnostics, Face ID, widgets, exports and support email, which reflect features in the current App.
