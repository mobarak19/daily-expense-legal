DailyExpences is built to track your spending — not you. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app touches, where it's stored, and what stays entirely on your device.

§01Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the DailyExpences iOS app ("the App"), developed by [Your Name / Studio Name] ("we," "us"). It describes how information is handled when you use the App. By using DailyExpences, you agree to the practices described here.

§02What We Collect

DailyExpences is designed to work entirely on your device. The App creates and stores the following, all locally:

CategoryExamples
Financial entries you addExpenses, categories, monthly budgets, recurring expense templates
Optional records you addLend & borrow entries, investment entries
App preferencesCurrency, appearance, week-start day, notification settings
Purchase statusWhether you hold a Pro subscription or lifetime unlock, verified via Apple

None of this requires creating an account, and none of it is tied to your name, email, or any other identifier — because we never ask for one.

§03What We Don't Collect

  • No account sign-up, email address, or password
  • No precise or approximate location data
  • No contacts, photos, or device identifiers beyond what Apple's App Store uses to process purchases
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs at this time
If a future version adds analytics, crash reporting, or ads, this policy will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the version number above.

§04Where Data Lives

All of your expense data is stored in a local database on your device (via Realm). It is not transmitted to our servers, because we don't operate any — DailyExpences does not sync your data to the cloud. If you use Apple's device backup (iCloud Backup / Finder backup), your App data may be included in that backup the same way any other app's data would be, subject to Apple's own policies.

§05Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details — Apple handles billing, receipts, and renewal, and manages that relationship directly with your Apple ID.

§06Notifications

Budget alerts and spending-summary reminders are scheduled locally on your device based on your own data and settings. They are not sent from a remote server. You can turn them off any time in the App's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

§07Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Since your financial entries never leave your device, there is nothing for us to share in the first place.

§08Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as the App is installed. Deleting DailyExpences from your device deletes its local database along with it. Because we don't hold a server-side copy, there is nothing further for us to delete on our end.

§09Children

DailyExpences is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the App does not collect personal information from any user, this applies equally to users of all ages.

§10Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Effective" date and version number at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes — such as introducing analytics, ads, or cloud sync — will be called out clearly rather than folded in silently.
