FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

How do I add an expense?

Tap the green + button on the Home screen and choose Add Expense. Enter the amount, a title and a category, then tap Save. You can also add one from the Lock Screen with the Quick Add widget.

How do I set a budget?

Tap the Monthly Budget card on Home. Enter a total for the month, turn on Budget Alerts and choose the percentage at which you want to be warned. You can also give each category its own budget.

How do I change the currency?

Go to Settings → Currency. You can choose USD, EUR, GBP, BDT or INR. Only the currency symbol changes. Amounts are not converted.

Can I add my own categories?

Yes. Tap + → Add Category, or create one while adding an expense. Choose a name, icon, color and an optional monthly budget.

Recurring and upcoming

What's the difference between recurring and upcoming expenses?

Recurring expenses are logged for you automatically each time they come due, like a streaming subscription. Upcoming expenses are bills you plan for. They remind you on the due date, and you tap Paid when you've paid them.

A recurring expense wasn't added. Why?

Recurring expenses are added when the app runs. If the app was closed on the due date, the missed expenses are added the next time you open it. Also check that notifications are allowed, because the app needs them to schedule repeats.

Widgets and notifications

How do I add a widget?

Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Daily Expenses, and pick a size.

Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, tap the widget area, and choose Spending at a Glance or Quick Add.

Why does my Lock Screen widget hide the amounts?

For privacy, iOS hides the amounts until your iPhone is unlocked. Unlock with Face ID or your passcode to see them.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Daily Expenses, then open the app's Settings → Notifications and make sure the reminders you want are turned on. Focus modes can also silence notifications.

Data and privacy

Is my data backed up or synced?

Your data is stored only on your iPhone. The app doesn't sync to the cloud, but its data is included in your iCloud or computer backup. Restoring that backup to a new iPhone brings your data back. You can also export CSV files from Settings → Export Data.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Clear All Data. This permanently deletes your expenses, categories, budgets and other records from the app. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.

How do I change my ad tracking choices?

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy Options to review your ad consent. You can also turn app tracking on or off in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Pro users don't see any ads.

Pro and security

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID you bought Pro with. Open the app, tap Upgrade, then tap Restore Purchases.

How do I turn on Face ID lock?

With Pro, go to Settings → Security and turn on App Lock to lock the whole app, or open Feature Lock to lock only certain screens. Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode must be set up on your iPhone first.

I can't open the app because Face ID isn't working.

The lock screen falls back to your iPhone passcode. Tap to authenticate again and enter your passcode when asked.
